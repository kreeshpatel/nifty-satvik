## Top 3 (EV for this book × cheapness)

### 1. Information-discreteness (FIP) as the seat-allocation key among *buyable* signals
- **Why EV:** 0150 shows the current key (CRS) orders tradeable outcomes at IC −0.004 and the A-set wastes
  ~¼ of seats. The book is 5 seats, so whatever decides who gets the cash is the highest-leverage
  decision left. FIP is a different feature source (sign-consistency of daily returns), never measured
  on the swing funnel.
- **Cheapest test (tier 0, zero trials):** on the 0150 Part-1 substrate (filled trades, train ≤2024-06-30,
  PIT membership), compute within-week rank IC of `ID = sign(ret_formation) × (%neg days − %pos days)`
  over the 63d before entry, week-clustered CI, plus the ranks-1–5-minus-rest R gap. The lab
  (`nq/lab/runs.py`) has no rank override key, so this is a scratch count, not a replay.
- **Must clear:** Law II (ranker ≠ rescue; 0132 wrong-signed ranker), Law I wall row 3 (path shape 0116
  flipped sign on the sealed set — FIP is a path feature, so a train-only positive is weak), activation
  bound (`verdict-machine` Gate 3) before any trial, and the 0150 seat-waste fix as the new baseline.

### 2. Live-vs-replay decision diff on adjusted vs unadjusted prices (Six-Defects audit)
- **Why EV:** zero multiplicity, protects real money now. Defects 2 and 6 map directly to us: engine
  levels are computed on adjusted history; users execute on Kite unadjusted prices, and live overlays
  live in `live_overlays`, which `load_frozen_cfg` deliberately drops.
- **Cheapest test (tier 1):** for every forward-wall week so far, replay the same week through the engine
  and diff signal set, grade, entry/stop levels and sizes against what the live cron published; separately,
  list every live position or signal whose name had an ex-date/split inside its lookback window and
  compare adjusted vs unadjusted stop levels. Also assert each live config flag is read on the order path.
- **Must clear:** reproduce-before-trust; `skills/portfolio-simulation` (live-vs-backtest divergence);
  existing `tests/test_replay_parity.py` scope (extend it, don't duplicate it).

### 3. De-gross with a fixed seat count: re-test vol-management on the live config
- **Why EV:** DD is the owner's second objective, and 0095's kill mechanism was *redeployment*

  PIT membership and config P.

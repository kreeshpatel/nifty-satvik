## #1 SIZING FACT: is the book a 20%-notional, 5-slot, equal-weight book?

**Method.** For every fill, notional = shares x fill price from the position log's first row for that name; sizing equity = the PRIOR session's equity (the engine sizes off `eq` carried from the previous close;
the very first live session uses EQ0 = 1,000,000); implied risk = notional / equity x (entry - initial stop) / entry. "At cap" = within 0.02 pp of 20%.

**(a) Live forward record (n = 10 fills, 2026-07-06..2026-10-12).** Notional / equity = **0.2000 on all 10 (100% at cap)**. Stop widths 4.1-10.0% (median 6.4%), so implied risk is
**0.83-2.00%, median 1.27%, mean 1.38%; none above 2%.** MCX hits exactly 2.00% (stop width at the 10% clamp). Cross-check against `attribution_ledger.csv` `position_size`:
10 names, 0.2000-0.2035 of prior equity (MCX 20.35%, CHOLAFIN 20.18%; the ledger's share counts differ slightly from the brain log for those two, and CHOLAFIN is in the ledger but not the log, GESHIP the reverse).
Live holds 4.79 names on average, never more than 5 (75 sessions).

**(b) Train base replay (n = 77 fills).** **77 / 77 at exactly 20.0%.** Stop width median 9.3%, **40.3% of fills have the stop clamped at the 10% max_risk**; implied risk 0.36-2.00%, median 1.86%, mean 1.65%.
Reading: `RISK = 2%` is a dead parameter in the live configuration, as the audit said (`0.02 / w >= 20%` for every `w <= 10%`), and the price of an R is not constant: the same 1R is 0.4% of equity on a 2% stop and 2.0% on a 10% stop.
**Correction to the "5-slot" framing:** the train book holds **7.06 names on average (max 10; 95% of sessions >= 5 names)**, because tranche sales free cash for new 20% fills while runners stay open. Live (a short window) holds <= 5. "5 slots" is true of fresh 20% fills, not of names held.

**(c) EXPLORATORY grid, 8 lab runs, train window, PIT membership.** Deflated Sharpe = Sharpe minus the expected maximum Sharpe of the meter's 8 trials (SR0 = 0.637 annual).
`nq.lab.runs.deflated_sharpe` convention (per-63-day-window basis, n_obs = 21).

## #7 COSTS: what the model leaves out, and the break-even

**Method.** (i) base ledger as the engine books it. (ii) subtract stamp duty 0.015% of each buy notional and a Rs 15.93 DP charge per distinct sell date per scrip (tranche sales and the final exit; end-of-sample marks excluded) from NAV on its date, path unchanged.
(iii) check the exit fill rule in the engine source; then add extra bp to stop exits only. Break-even: extra bp per side on EVERY leg, two ways (a) frozen-path deduction, (b) full engine re-run with `_cost_leg` patched in-process (never written to disk; unpatched config label LIVE BOOK).

- **(i)** current model: CAGR 20.47%, Sharpe 1.025, MaxDD -40.1%, net P&L Rs 17.72 lakh. Modelled cost per leg is brokerage 3 bp + STT 10 bp + ADV-tier slippage 5 / 22 / 40 bp.
- **(ii)** stamp Rs 3,278 + DP Rs 2,103 (132 sell scrip-days) over 5.5 years: **CAGR -4.3 bp (20.42%), Sharpe -0.003.** Negligible.
- **(iii)** The engine already fills a stop exit **at the next session's open** (weekly close <= stop queues a `pending` exit, filled at `s["o"][i]`), so the gap between the stop and the fill is **already in the P&L: the gap itself adds 0.**
  What is not modelled is extra impact when selling into a gap (same bp as any exit). Sensitivity, on the 32 stop exits only: +25 / +50 / +100 / +200 bp gives **-13 / -27 / -54 / -109 bp CAGR** (Rs 17k / 34k / 68k / 136k).
- **Break-even (a) frozen path:** CAGR 20.47% -> 18.7% at +50 bp, 16.8% at +100, 12.6% at +200, 7.6% at +300. CAGR **halves at +247 bp per side** and **reaches zero at +412 bp per side** (Sharpe 1.025 -> 0.79 at +100 bp).
- **Break-even (b) full re-run:** CAGR by extra bp per side = 0: 20.5%, 5: 20.2%, 10: 19.9%, 20: 19.3%, 30: 20.2%, 50: 25.1%, 75: 23.7%, 100: 17.8%, 150: 12.8%, 200: 5.7%, 300: 7.2%.
  **Not monotone** (Sharpe ranges 0.92-1.22 across 0-100 bp): a small cost change re-orders which names win scarce cash (the same cash contention as #9) and the whole path moves. Interpolated halving ~ +168 bp; read it as order of magnitude only.
- **Reading.** The book tolerates roughly 2% of extra cost per side before CAGR halves, so costs are not the binding fragility. The same experiment measures this book's path noise: a +50 bp cost *increase* produces a +4.6 pp *higher* CAGR. That noise floor is the number to carry into every Sharpe comparison.

## #3 GAP EXITS: stops that fill through the stop

**Method.** Stop exits (`stop`, `stop_part`) from the base ledger; the live scaled exit never ratchets, so `stop0` is the stop. Beyond-stop R = (stop0 - exit price) / (entry - stop0), per unit of price. Live: `attribution_ledger.csv` `gap_through` and `beyond_stop_r` (its own definition, negative).

| | stop exits | filled through | share | beyond-stop R mean / median / p90 / max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| train (2019-01..2024-06) | 32 of 77 trades | **29** | **90.6%** | **0.90 / 0.56 / 2.30 / 4.37**; 15 beyond 0.5R, 7 beyond 1R |
| live cards (forward from 2026-07-04) | 22 | 20 | 90.9% | -0.65 / -0.42 / n/a / worst -2.76 (ledger sign) |
| live book names only | 4 | 4 | 100% | -0.05, -0.20, -0.46, -0.56 |

The mean ledger R of a train stop exit is **-1.54R against a designed -1R.** Reading: because the stop is decided on the weekly close and filled at the next open, a fill through the stop is the norm (about nine in ten), not a tail; the average stop exit gives up about 0.5R more than designed. The live sample is card-level and small, and shows the same share; its R scale is a different definition, so compare shares, not R. This is the number a hard-stop or gap-aware sizing test would have to beat.

## #10 FREEZE: how often would the live corporate-action hold bite in history?

**Method.** For every session after entry up to and including the exit session of each of the 77 train trades, flag open or close <= -15% vs the prior close (`CA_HOLD_DROP`). Engine order respected: an open-leg trigger precedes a queued fill (freeze blocks it); a close-leg trigger follows the day's fills.
"Registered" = in `data/corporate_actions_demerger_register.csv`.

**Result.** **5 trigger sessions in 9,552 held sessions = 0.91 per year, 4 name-episodes, 0 in the demerger register (all genuine crashes, so each would be a human review, not a re-base).**
- JSL 2020-03-12 (close -17.0%): frozen; the book exited within 3 sessions, so the mechanical exit would have waited for review.
- VBL 2020-03-23 (open -20.0%): a queued `sma_break` exit was filled at that open; the open-leg freeze would have **blocked** it.
- MUTHOOTFIN 2020-03-23 (open -10.0%, close -16.5%): exit filled at the open before the close check, so it was **booked**, not frozen.
- ATGL 2023-01-27 (close -20.0%): frozen; the 2023-01-30 stop exit (open -14.6%) would have been **blocked**.

So **3 of 4 episodes would be frozen instead of booked, 1 booked.** Reading: the hold bites about once a year on this universe and every historical hit is a real crash (March 2020, Adani), which is the cost the owner accepted; research replays book those mechanically at the gap open and so carry them in the train P&L, whereas live waits for a human. n is 4 episodes: a rate, not a P&L claim.

## #9 ENTRY WINDOW: how often does an A signal never fill, and on which weekday

**Method.** Re-derive from the engine's own `prep_weekly_rank` output the live mechanics for every Grade-A signal 2019-01..2024-06: membership gate at activation, then each session of the next ISO week:
fill if `lo < open < hi` (strict) and `open <= SMA x 1.20`. Reconciled to the engine: the 740 eligible signals = **526 fills in the uncapped lab run + 214 where the ticker was already held; 0 unexplained.**

The mirror of "no open lands in the band" is therefore **8.3% (gap-up) plus 9.8% (ext cap) = 18.1% of activated A signals**.

**Capital is the larger filter, not the band:** of 740 eligible signals the capped book makes 77 fills (10.4%): 36 blocked because the ticker was held, 8 delayed fills, and the rest not funded (cash / same-day CRS priority).
Reading: the entry window as written is almost a Monday window (Tue-Fri add 22% of fills); a Monday-only test would drop about a fifth of eligible fills, and the band/ext-cap voids (18%) are small next to the 90% that cash decides. Descriptive only.

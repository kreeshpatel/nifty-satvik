**Class: VERIFICATION. Zero trials, zero screens. Counts frozen at screens 16 · sealed opens 1 ·
n_trials 138.** No verdict is reversed, revived, or re-adjudicated. Generated by
[`scripts/build_transferability_register.py`](../../scripts/build_transferability_register.py)
— edit the classification there, not this file.

A binder input, and the third audit layer. The first asked whether the *input* was true
(`FOUNDATION_AUDIT.md`); the second asked whether the *instrument* could resolve what it claimed
(`POWER_READJUDICATION.md`). This one asks the question both of those leave open: **a verdict can
be correctly measured on a real book with adequate power and still not apply to the thing someone
cites it against.**

## The rule this register enforces

> **A verdict measured on the LH momentum book does NOT bind the swing book unless the mechanism
> is argued explicitly.** Same for substrate→book, sleeve→book, and ancestor-config→live-config.

This is not a theoretical worry. The corpus contains a **measured** instance: `O-009` vol-target
was **PROMOTED** on LH-momentum and shipped to paper, then ported verbatim to the swing book by
`0095` and **KILLED** there at ΔSharpe −0.398. The mechanism inverts on a cash-constrained book.
A second instance is intra-family: the CRS gate lost on the daily six-step book (`0028`, −0.14)
and helped on the weekly one (`0036`) — finding 0036 says so in its own text.

on IC, per-trade R, per-year sign counts, or a multi-leg pre-committed bar. So #4 is a statement
about the subset that CAN be tested against the band, not about the corpus.

And the band itself does not transfer cleanly. **±0.302 was derived on the swing book** (±10R/yr noise floor against a 1.617σ book return). Applying it to LH-momentum rows is itself an
untested transfer. Of the 23 in-band rows, **12 were
measured on a book the band actually describes**; the remaining 11 are flagged, not counted as settled.
**So the defensible reading of #4 is a range: 12–23.**

## Guard — what this register does NOT do

**Nothing is revived.** A verdict that fails transferability becomes **UNTESTED-HERE**, which is a
statement about the evidence, not an invitation. It is *not* OPEN-FOR-RETEST. Re-testing any of
these still requires a pre-registration, a declared re-open key from {new data, new feature
source, new sub-period, new formulation}, and `n_trials` pricing at the trunk. A row moving from
`YES` to `NO` in the swing column removes a *citation*, not a *cost*.

**40 rows** are UNTESTED-HERE against both the live book and breadth-50.
That is the register's main product: a list of things the programme knows about a book it no
longer runs, which have been carrying rhetorical weight they were never measured to carry.

## Root-cause readout

The corpus was built book-by-book as the programme's vehicle changed underneath it — LH momentum,
then an external swing arc, then the weekly line, then a substrate, then two exit regimes in two
days. Each verdict was correct where it was measured. The registry recorded *what* was decided and
*why*, but never *on what* — so a session searching it before proposing gets hits that look
binding and are not. `O-009` is the proof: the same lever, PROMOTED on one book and KILLED on
another, sits in the same ledger with no column distinguishing them until now.
